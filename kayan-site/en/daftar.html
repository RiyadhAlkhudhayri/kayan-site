Kayan Ledger, our system for running orders and financing

Order, delivery, invoice and payment, in one system.

We built Kayan Ledger after years of running supply by hand. Every step issues its own document, and every riyal in the fund is matched by a real order.

Three views of a single order

End customer

Restaurant, hotel, caterer

Orders for every branch from one account at agreed prices, picks the branch and delivery time, tracks orders, and pays through the platform.

Supplier

Fruit and vegetable suppliers

Receives each order with its items and quantities, prepares it, assigns a driver, prints the delivery note, and tracks every order to completion.

Driver

Delivery to the customer

Receives the delivery job with its address and time slot, delivers the order, and confirms delivery at the customer’s location.

An order’s journey on Kayan Ledger

  1. 1OrderThe customer places and confirms the order.
  2. 2PreparationThe supplier prepares it and assigns a driver.
  3. 3DeliveryThe driver delivers and records proof of delivery.
  4. 4InvoiceIssued automatically and compliant with ZATCA requirements.
  5. 5PaymentWe pay the supplier and collect from the customer on the due date.

Why Kayan Ledger is the foundation for financing

Financing was never possible in this sector because transactions were on paper or verbal. Kayan Ledger turns every order into a verified record that can be built on.

An invoice for every order
Issued automatically and compliant with ZATCA requirements, with no manual entry.

Documented proof of delivery
A delivery note listing items and quantities, plus electronic confirmation from the driver.

A real-time view of the fund
We know where every riyal is: available cash, delivered goods, or an invoice within its term.
