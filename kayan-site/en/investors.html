How the fund works

Step 1

Deposit

You invest in the Kayan Fund as the capital provider (rab al-mal), with Kayan as the manager (mudarib).

Step 2

Deployment

The capital pays suppliers and farmers, and we collect from customers within 30 to 45 days.

Step 3

Return

The money returns with its profit and is redeployed, with a monthly statement after closing and review.

Know where your money is working

Because every order runs through Kayan Ledger, we can see the capital at every stage: available cash, delivered goods, and invoices within their term. Any delay shows up immediately.

Mudarabah
The investor provides the capital and Kayan manages it. Profit is shared at agreed ratios of actual profit, never a fixed amount.

Subject of the contract
Goods that are bought and sold, not paper debt.

Reference
AAOIFI Shariah Standard No. 13 (Mudarabah).
