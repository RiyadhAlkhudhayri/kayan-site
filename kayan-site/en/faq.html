FAQ

Short answers to the questions we hear most about Kayan.

About Kayan

What is Kayan?

Kayan finances food supply chains: we pay suppliers and farmers early, collect from the end customer on the due date, and record every step on Kayan Ledger.

What is Kayan Ledger?

The system every order runs through: the customer orders, the supplier prepares and delivers, delivery is recorded, the invoice is issued, and then payment is made. That record is what makes financing possible.

Is the financing Shariah-compliant?

Yes. We finance real trade in goods that are bought and sold, not interest-bearing loans: Mudarabah for investors under AAOIFI Shariah Standard No. 13, and Salam for farmers under Standard No. 10.

For suppliers and end customers

When does the supplier get paid?

When delivery is confirmed on Kayan Ledger, instead of waiting 45 to 90 days for the restaurant or hotel to pay.

What does Kayan charge for early payment?

An agreed percentage of the order value, set with the supplier in the contract before work begins.

Does the end customer pay fees for buying on deferred terms?

No. The customer pays the same order value on the due date, with no added fees.

How does a restaurant or hotel open an account?

Contact us, and we will open a Kayan Ledger account for you and link it to your branches and suppliers.

For farmers

How does a farmer get paid for their crop?

In one of two ways: direct payment on delivery, or we buy the crop upfront before harvest under a Salam contract.

What is a Salam contract?

A Shariah-compliant contract in which we pay the full price of the crop upfront, and the farmer commits to deliver it to the agreed specifications on the agreed date.

For investors

Is the return guaranteed?

No. The return is a target. Investing under a Mudarabah contract carries the possibility of both profit and loss, and past results do not guarantee future results.

How long is a capital cycle?

Usually 30 to 45 days from purchase to collection, after which the money is put back to work in a new cycle.

How do I know where my money is working?

Every order is recorded on Kayan Ledger, so we know where the capital is at every stage, and you receive a statement after each closing and review.
