About us

Kayan finances agricultural and food supply chains.

We finance the period between delivering goods and collecting payment, so farmers and suppliers get paid early, end customers buy on deferred terms, and investors earn from real trade.

The problem

Goods move every day, but money is weeks behind

The supplier waits
They deliver to a restaurant or hotel today and are only paid 45 to 90 days later, funding their customers out of their own pocket the whole time.

The farmer waits
They hand their crop to a broker or trader and wait weeks, while they need cash for the next season.

And financing is missing
Most transactions are on paper or verbal, so there is no reliable record any financier can depend on.

What we do

Three jobs in one cycle

1

We pay early

Suppliers on confirmed delivery for an agreed fee, and farmers on delivery or upfront under a Salam contract.

2

We record every order

On Kayan Ledger: the order, the delivery, the invoice and the payment. Every riyal we finance is matched by a real order.

3

We collect on time

From the end customer on the due date. The money then returns with its profit and goes back to work.

Our experience

We built Kayan from inside the market

For four years we have worked inside the fruit and vegetable market, with a network of more than 100 farms. That daily work showed us exactly where cash gets stuck, so we built Kayan Ledger to record orders, and Kayan to finance them.

Our principles

Financing real trade, not loans

Shariah-compliant

Mudarabah for investors and Salam for farmers, following AAOIFI Shariah Standards: No. 13 for Mudarabah and No. 10 for Salam.

Real goods

Every contract is backed by goods that are bought, sold and delivered, not by paper debt.

Full transparency

We know where every riyal is: available cash, delivered goods, or an invoice within its term.
