Your crop deserves to be paid for on time.

Choose what suits you: direct payment on delivery, or we buy your crop upfront under a Salam contract.

Two options for farmers

Option 1

Direct payment

Deliver your crop and we pay you directly, instead of waiting weeks for a broker or trader.

  • A clear price agreed before delivery
  • Cash for your next growing cycle

Option 2

Salam contract

We buy your crop before harvest: we pay the full price upfront, and you deliver the crop to the agreed specifications on time.

  • Funding for inputs and labor before the season
  • A guaranteed sale for your crop at a known price
  • A Shariah contract: a commitment to deliver goods, not to repay cash with interest

How to get started

Step 1

Step 3

You get paid

On delivery, or upfront if you choose Salam.
